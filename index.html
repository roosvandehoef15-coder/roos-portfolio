About

I’m interested in how design can drive change — from systems-level strategy to visual storytelling and creative communication. My master’s in Strategic Product Design at TU Delft has inspired me to turn complex problems into tangible solutions, visuals, and stories. Inside and outside of my studies, curiosity fuels everything I create. I love connecting the dots between systems, needs, and ideas to deliver solutions that are new, creative, and fitting. In both creative and strategic problem-solving, I involve people, planet, profit — and personality, because everything tells a story.

“I see uncertainty as the most exciting design material.”
